Building on KitsuneUI

Make your mod's windows wear the glass

KitsuneUI is a theme for 7 Days to Die, and also a small framework. Your windows can use its colours and glass, follow each player's look settings, and join Move HUD. Players without KitsuneUI see your mod exactly as they do today.

The look EAC on

KitsuneUI: XML and images only. Colours, sprites and building blocks.

Move HUD EAC off

KitsuneUI_Settings, a DLL: players drag, resize and hide your HUD pieces.

1. The one rule: wrap it in a conditional

Use KitsuneUI's names only inside a mod_loaded check. A [kuGold] or a ku_panel outside one breaks your window for everyone who doesn't run KitsuneUI.

Config/XUi/windows.xml
<configs>
  <!-- your mod's own patches stay out here, as they are -->
  <conditional>
    <if cond="mod_loaded('KitsuneUI')">
      <!-- KitsuneUI look here -->
    </if>
  </conditional>
</configs>
Move HUDUse mod_loaded('KitsuneUI_Settings') for ku_move_handle. A player can run the look without the DLL.
No dependencyYou don't need to list KitsuneUI as a requirement. Players who don't have it get your mod exactly as you made it.

4. Building blocks

Templates for the parts most windows need. All of them take pos and depth.

<ku_window_body />the glass body under a 43-tall header, filling the rest of the window (top to change where it starts)
<ku_section text_key="..." width="400" />a gold section title with a rule under it
<ku_row width="400" height="34" />a rounded cell; alt="true" for alternate rows
<ku_toggle_row name="tglThing" text_key="..." />a glass toggle with its label (the label is tglThingText)

Example: a glass window

Inside your mod_loaded check
<!-- the glass body behind everything -->
<prepend xpath="/windows/window[@name='myWindow']">
  <ku_window_body />
</prepend>
<!-- the old grey background goes -->
<set xpath="/windows/window[@name='myWindow']//sprite[@name='background']/@color">[transparent]</set>
<!-- values in gold -->
<setattribute xpath="/windows/window[@name='myWindow']//label[@name='value']" name="color">[kuGold]</setattribute>

5. Move HUD

Put a ku_move_handle in your HUD window and it joins Move HUD: players drag it, resize it (60 to 160%), hide it, snap it to the grid, and save it in their layouts. It's kept on screen, saved per player as move.<your window name>, and reset with everything else.

Config/XUi/windows.xml
<conditional>
  <if cond="mod_loaded('KitsuneUI_Settings')">
    <append xpath="/windows/window[@name='myHudStrip']">
      <ku_move_handle pos="0,0"
        width="320" height="48"
        label_key="myHudStripName" />
    </append>
  </if>
</conditional>
Now playingWasteland Radio · Dusty Roads
pos, width, heightthe outline, in the window's coordinates; match your piece
label_keyyour localization key for the piece's name on the outline (or label for plain text)
groupoptional; several handles with the same group move one piece
open_for_moving="true"for a window that's only open now and then (a now-playing box, a notification); Move HUD opens it while it's on, so there's something to drag, and closes it after
One window eachYour piece is moved by its window, so give it a window of its own.

6. Built with it: KitsuneRadio

KitsuneRadio's tuner and now-playing strip wear the glass when KitsuneUI is on, and the strip joins Move HUD. This is its real XML, shortened. Without KitsuneUI none of it applies and the radio looks as it always has.

KitsuneRadio/Config/XUi_InGame/windows.xml
<conditional>
  <if cond="mod_loaded('KitsuneUI')">
    <prepend xpath="/windows/window[@name='kitsuneRadioTuner']">
      <ku_window_body />
    </prepend>
    <setattribute xpath="/windows/window[@name='kitsuneRadioTuner']//label[@name='kr_now']" name="color">[kuGold]</setattribute>
    <!-- the station buttons: glass buttons -->
    <set xpath="/windows/window[@name='kitsuneRadioTuner']//rect[contains(@name, 'Box')]/button/@sprite">ku_round_sm</set>
    <set xpath="/windows/window[@name='kitsuneRadioTuner']//rect[contains(@name, 'Box')]/button/@defaultcolor">[kuBtn]</set>
    <set xpath="/windows/window[@name='kitsuneRadioTuner']//rect[contains(@name, 'Box')]/button/@hovercolor">[kuBtnHover]</set>
    <!-- the now-playing strip: a small glass panel -->
    <set xpath="/windows/window[@name='kitsuneRadioStrip']/sprite[@depth='1']/@sprite">ku_panel_sm</set>
    <setattribute xpath="/windows/window[@name='kitsuneRadioStrip']/label[@name='kr_stripStation']" name="color">[kuTextBright]</setattribute>
  </if>
</conditional>

<conditional>

7. Promises

Names stay put

Everything in this guide keeps its name and meaning for all of 1.x. New names may come in minor releases; removing or renaming one would be KitsuneUI 2.0.

Checked every release

A script checks all 52 public names before each release, so nothing slips. Anything not in this guide is internal and can change.

Your licence stays yours

Using these names and templates doesn't make your mod a changed KitsuneUI. NOTICE.md says so in writing. Copying KitsuneUI's own files or art is covered by its licence.

8. Tips

  • Test both ways. Load your mod once with KitsuneUI and once without. The second run is the one that catches a name outside the conditional.
  • set or setattribute? set only changes an attribute that's already there. If your label has no color yet, use setattribute.
  • Grey where you expected glass? Your window's own background sprite is drawn over the glass. Set its colour to [transparent].
  • Faded text inside a glass box? The game multiplies a child's alpha by its parent's, so text inside a see-through sprite fades with it. Keep the container's own sprite opaque.
  • Something missing? Open an issue on GitHub with the framework label, or ask on the Nexus page.
