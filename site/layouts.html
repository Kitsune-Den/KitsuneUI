Layouts to try

A layout is one line of text. Copy it, press Import in the game, and your HUD changes. These need the KitsuneUI Settings add-on, which runs with EAC off.

  1. Save yours. In the game, open KitsuneUI Settings, then Profiles, and save your layout under a name.
  2. Copy one. Press Copy on a layout below.
  3. Import it. Press Import in Profiles. Load your saved profile to go back.

What changesImport replaces your positions, sizes and hidden pieces: anything the layout doesn't mention goes back to its default. Other settings change only if the layout names them. KitsuneUI accepts its own settings and nothing else.
Other screensEach piece remembers how far it sits from its own edge of the screen, so on a wider or narrower monitor it stays by that edge. If one ends up off screen, open Move HUD and it comes back.
